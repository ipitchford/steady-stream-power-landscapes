# Status of version 1.3.0-candidate

The manuscripts present analytical proofs for the classes stated in the [claim ledger](CLAIM_LEDGER.json). They remain a research candidate with AI-assisted review, no formal proof certificate, no established external specialist acceptance, and provisional originality. The full supplied Problem 5 is partially resolved.

## What is established within the manuscripts

- General-domain small-erosion steady existence for positive Hölder forcing and all positive exponents.
- Local uniqueness and constructive estimates with the additional constant-forcing, exponent-sum and flow-margin hypotheses.
- Consolidated sufficient smallness inequalities and a conditional inexact-iteration convergence theorem with finite inner stopping.
- All-parameter regular radial steadiness, radial evolution for slope exponent at least one, and radial singular-limit classifications.
- Exact full-system wet–dry/wet-up subclasses and specified channel identities, profile repair and regular-class compatibility obstruction.

“Proof presented” identifies the mathematical content and hypotheses. It is not shorthand for proof-assistant certification, empirical validation, or an independent human reproduction.

## Remaining scientific questions

Arbitrary-erosion nonradial existence, uniqueness outside the restricted branch, nonradial dynamics and transverse stability, and general wet–dry coupled evolution remain open here. The channel conjecture for suitable strictly positive decaying data is neither proved nor refuted. The compact-data obstruction does not exclude an initial layer or a weaker class.

No admissible numerical `K_*` or certified nonradial branch has been instantiated. The ellipse's geometric margin is exact, but invariant-ball radii, elliptic constants and all continuum numerical errors still require bounds for such an application. These are future research tasks, not missing claims silently supplied by small sampled residuals.
